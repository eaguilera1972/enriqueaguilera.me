About

Enrique Aguilera is a technology entrepreneur and the Chief Executive Officer of Diez Mobile US, Inc., a mobile carrier serving the Latino community in the United States. He is based in Boston.

He began his career in digital marketing, first in search engine optimization and then in paid search. At Panda Security he ran the online business worldwide before leading sales and marketing for the company’s global consumer business.

In 2014 he co-founded Quarizmi AdTech in Cambridge, Massachusetts. The company builds proprietary machine learning algorithms that automate Google Ads optimization at scale. As CEO, he set the product vision, guided engineering, and managed relationships with Google and technology partners, growing Quarizmi to serve clients around the world.

Today he applies that experience to telecommunications, with a focus on how AI and data can change the way carriers operate and compete. He works in English and Spanish and holds a Bachelor of Science from Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University.
